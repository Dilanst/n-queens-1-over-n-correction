\subsection{Status of each claim}\label{sec:claim-ledger}
The label \status{D} identifies proved results.
We use \status{O} for an exact computer check or a rigorous interval,
\status{N} for a numerical estimate, and \status{D if C} for a bound proved under the stated
condition. The keys under ``Source'' refer to the tables in the supplement on the proof,
rooks, and the numerical calculation.

The proof of the limit uses the rational identities for the pair and the square. Richardson
extrapolation is used in evaluating the formula for $\cerr$. The symbolic comparison of 42 pieces
provides an independent check of the cancellation at the endpoint.
