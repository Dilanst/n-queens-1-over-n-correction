\subsection{Status of each claim}\label{sec:claim-ledger}
The label \status{D} identifies proved results. We use \status{O} for an exact computer check or a
rigorous interval, \status{N} for a numerical estimate, and \status{D if C} for a bound proved under the
stated condition. The entries in the “Source” column refer to the tables in the supplement on the proof,
the rook problem, and the numerical computation.

The proof of the limit is computer-assisted at two steps. The rational computation in
Appendix~\ref{app:pair} verifies the degree-four coefficients of the pair and the square. The analytic
bounds on their remainders are uniform in the stationary class. Richardson extrapolation is used in
evaluating the formula for $\cerr$. The symbolic comparison of 42 pieces provides an independent check of
the cancellation at the endpoint.
